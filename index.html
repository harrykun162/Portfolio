Hi, I'm Loc.

I am a guy who has spent a lot of time wondering about my existence in this world. I did not really have one clear interest growing up, but whenever I started something, I always chased it to the end. I played piano and performed at city events, trained in taekwondo and earned my black belt, and even won the Australian Esport League last year through competitive gaming.

Then came data science, which I think of as a universe full of "whys". That is what made me fall in love with it. There is never really an endpoint. Every result opens the door to more questions: why it happened, how we should use it, what it means for the real world, and how we can improve from there. I want to keep applying what I know in real projects and grow into opportunities in data science and data analytics.
